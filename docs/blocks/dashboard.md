# dashboard

Status: done for the shipped scope; contracts v0.10, deployed 2026-09-27. Three views in the header (D-31): **Chain** - one block per session (`xs`) headed by who connected, the proof (`identity.js`, D-29), the tracking ids and the counts, its connection folded into one line, then its episodes and context rows, its end; every call row says `in` and `out`, an open call prints its tracking line and labels its panes input and output, and every raw envelope is drawn as tracking first and `data` second (C1, C2, D-30); **Account** - the persona's money for a signed-in viewer (`panel-account.js`); **Overview** - counts across everything the viewer may read (`panel-overview.js`, D-32), the public lane's landing page. Chain mode keeps the spine of D-17 (REQUEST / INSIDE / RESPONSE per call), the Possibility space, the retired board (D-18). Open: the Bytes drawer (D-19), phone polish and a deep link to one call, now in the second-pass plan (D-34).

## Purpose
The X-ray SPA: vanilla HTML, CSS and ES modules - no bundler, no framework, no npm dependency of its own, nothing imported from `src/`.
It renders the eight numbered panels of `docs/XRAY_EVENT_MODEL.md`, plus the connect screen, the now strip and the persona card, from the event stream, live or from the recorded fixture.

Every panel is a pure `render*(model)` with `model = {store, view, now}`; `now` is injected, never read from the clock. `app.js` gates the two big panels behind a signature and repaints them only when it changes: the timeline on last id, size, mode, actor focus, open JSON, the open state (`openSignature()` = depth, drawer, aside and every explicit choice except the `cat:` keys), filter, selection, session, pause, follow, pending count and `view.schemaCheckRevision`; the detail panel on last id, size, mode, tab, selection, session, drawer, pause, open JSON, connection state, backfill, the Possibility space's `cat:` choices (`catalogOpenSignature()`) and `view.schemaCheckRevision`. `openOrder` and `lastToggled` are in neither, on purpose. - Actions: `toggle-open` (arg the key, **arg2 the state the panel drew**, `'1'` open / `'0'` closed, so a click always undoes what the reader can see), `set-depth` (arg one of `overview` / `calls` / `open` / `inside`; it clears the `call:` choices only, so a folded episode, an open connection block and the toolbar's own state survive), `collapse-all` (arg `all`, an `xs` or `ep:<first call key>`; the episode's button lists its calls in arg2), `close-drawer`, `toggle-aside`. `window.__xray.act(action, arg, arg2)` forwards both arguments.
- Opening sets `view.follow = false` and `view.lastToggled`, which `revealLastToggled()` consumes with one `scrollIntoView({block: 'nearest'})` behind a `programmaticScroll` flag - without it that scroll re-arms the tail-follow and the next event scrolls the reader off the block they just opened. Closing forgets every key nested under the one that closed and the `view.json` state of the viewers it owned (`viewerPrefixesOf`).
- `Esc`, in order: blur the filter if the reader is typing, else close the block opened last (`escTarget`), else close the drawer, else clear the selection and the actor lens. The footer says `/ filter · Esc close`.
- `prune(view, store)` runs before every timeline repaint: it drops the choices whose call, session or listing the store no longer knows, drops their viewer state, then caps `view.open` at 400 entries, oldest first over `openOrder` - which lists **open and closed** entries, so a reader who closed hundreds of rows evicts those too. It is held while `view.backfilling` is true and before the first event has been applied, so a deep link or a replay is not pruned away on the first paint.
- `collapseUnder` at a depth whose default is an open call cannot just drop the choices: given the members (the episode's button passes them) it writes an explicit `closed` for each, and `all` steps the depth back to `calls`.
- Selection and expansion are independent: `select-call` (the `⤢` button, `.call-bytes`) feeds the inspector and expands nothing; `.call-row.is-selected` marks the inspector's subject, `[aria-expanded="true"]` marks what is open.
- **The row** (`renderCallRow`, `public/panel-call.js`). One line per call, whatever else is on screen. Left to right: the `ui.disclosure` head - `#<JSON-RPC id>`, clock, tool, status, duration, the actor dots - then the in-digest, a `→`, the out-digest, and `.call-bytes` as a **sibling** of the head, never nested in it. The in-digest is the model's arguments verbatim in the model colour, minus the rationale, which becomes a `+rationale` chip carrying the sentence in its `title` (or a warn-toned `no rationale` chip, A-06). The out-digest is the head of the recorded result text verbatim in the server colour, then the size fact (`486 sent · 69 kept` / `36 sent · 33 kept` / `waiting` / `no text`). **Both halves are recorded bytes.** A page-derived caption - `6 rows`, `table load_x`, `audit aud_0002` - never appears there: those live on the connectors and the thread heads, in the dashed page colour, where they can be seen for what they are. A denied call's in-digest is the server-rail sentence `requestHop().denied` returns, never the model rail. The digest asks for 40rem, so a narrow spine gives it a line of its own rather than shrinking both halves until the values disappear.
- **The numbering rule.** The JSON-RPC id is the only number a call is counted by. The per-episode step ordinal is gone from the rows and from the connectors, which now say `from #N` (or `from episode N` when the link crosses one).
- **The triptych** (`renderCallOpen`). Opened in place under the row, as a desktop grid `minmax(0,1fr) minmax(8rem,14rem) minmax(0,1fr)`; the opened INSIDE cards span `grid-column: 1 / -1` under the two wire panes, and the whole grid becomes one column under 900px. Titles are `REQUEST · tools/call · id <n> · as it arrived`, `INSIDE · this server and our engine`, `RESPONSE · result of id <n> · as it left`. **Nothing the server did on receipt - the scope check, the redaction, recording the intent - may appear inside REQUEST; it belongs to INSIDE.** Every pane cell carries `min-width: 0` and `overflow-wrap: anywhere`; the only thing that scrolls is a `.code[id]` block.
- **The footer rule.** Both wire panes always print the footer `hops.js` builds (`rebuilt by this page from tool.call.started #197: …`), and both offer a `raw <event type> #N` disclosure holding the whole envelope. A pane without its footer would be a claim with no provenance.
- Episode rule (`chain.js`): a call linked to anything already in the current episode always extends it, whatever the clock says. An unlinked call opens a new episode when the `intent.inferred` workflow in force changed, when more than 45 s passed since the previous call finished, or when the previous call was a closing tool (`clear_table`, `lock_or_unlock_card`, a confirmed `create_transfer`); otherwise it extends. Inside an episode, a call joins the thread of whatever it is linked to, so several pieces of work running at once are drawn as separate chains rather than one interleaved column.
- Episodes are the dashboard's grouping, not a record of the conversation. Every header carries the sentence `Grouped by this dashboard, not by the server, which never sees the conversation` (`EPISODE_HONESTY`) with no way to fold it away, and a `why` disclosure (`ep:<first call key>/why`) holding the full `groupingSentence` for that particular group. The workflow keeps the "inferred by this server, not by the model" tag, and the quoted rationale is labelled as the model's own words. The header prints `scored 0.85`, never a percentage: `confidence` is how far the winning workflow beat the runner-up (`0.4 + 0.45 x margin`, clamped, in `src/tools/intent.ts`), so only `0.2` for `unknown` and `0.40` to `0.85` are reachable.
- Five actors answer "who authored this" (`provenance.js`): `agent` the client app, `model` the model itself, `server` this bank server, `engine` the fake core banking and the scratch SQL, `page` derived in this browser. `actorOfEvent` reads a static table, except `tool.call.cancelled` (`agent` only when the reason is `client_cancelled`) and `protocol.error` (`server` only for `-32603`).
- The legend is drawn in chain mode only: five chips, `data-action="set-actor-focus"` with the actor as the arg. Clicking one sets `view.actorFocus` and dims (`.is-dimmed`) every `[data-actor]` mark the other four drew - the digest values, the actor dots, the wire rows, the card lines, the notes and the pills; clicking the focused chip again, or "Show all again", clears it. `page` is a first-class rail now (the implied `method` row, the locators, the ports, the footers, the ratios), and it keeps the connector pill, the episode grouping sentence and the context row marks lit as well - the one lens that separates what was recorded from what was worked out. `model` keeps the episode's quoted rationale lit for the same reason. A lens, not a filter: nothing leaves the page, which is why the check counts marks before and after. It is part of the timeline repaint signature and deliberately not remembered in `localStorage` - it is a way of reading one call, not a setting.
- JSON viewer (`json-view.js`): the raw envelope, structured content and `_meta` in the inspector, the capability sets in Session and auth, the `_meta`, structured content and raw envelopes of an open call, the raw descriptor in the Possibility space, and every non-string argument value. What is open lives in `view.json`, keyed by path - the viewer's `id` plus each key escaped with `encodeURIComponent` and then the seven characters it leaves behind (`.!~*'()`), joined with dots - so it survives a repaint; a key named `a.b` cannot collide with the nested `a` -> `b`, a key named `x!all` cannot toggle the sibling `x`, and `viewerId` builds an id that holds no dot. Suffixed keys hang extra state off a path: `!all-open` (the whole viewer), `!all` (show every array item), `!full` (the whole string), `!raw` (the exact text instead of the tree), `!copied` (what Copy just did). A branch is open when there is an explicit choice for it, else when the viewer-wide `!all-open` says so, else while its depth is under 2 and it holds at most 24 children. Actions: `toggle-json` (arg = the path), `json-open-all` and `json-close-all` (arg = the viewer id; both drop every per-node choice under it first), `copy-json`. Past `max` (200,000 characters) `safeJson` cuts and says so, and the button then reads "Copy part" instead of promising the whole.
- The JSON viewers of one call and the keys that own them (`viewerPrefixesOf`, `public/open-state.js`): the arguments are `step-args`, `_meta` is `step-meta` and the raw request envelope `step-args…-raw`, all under `<call>/req`; the structured content is `step-result` and the raw response envelope `step-result…-raw`, both under `<call>/res`; a card's raw envelope is `step-child-<event id>` under `<call>/inside/<event id>`. Closing a pane therefore forgets what its viewers had open, including the argument tree when the raw request block is closed.
- A viewer with no title whose JSON is under 400 characters draws no toolbar (`is-compact`): four buttons over a five-item array of column names is more chrome than content. Copy reads the hidden `<pre id="<id>-raw">` that is always in the DOM behind the tree, so what lands on the clipboard is exactly what arrived rather than a rendering of it; `navigator.clipboard` needs a secure context, so over plain HTTP the textarea plus `execCommand` fallback is the path that runs, and the button itself says `Copied` or `Blocked` for 1.6 s.
- Repaint gates: the timeline and the detail panel each repaint only when their own signature changes (`timelineSignature()` / `detailSignature()`, both carrying `jsonSignature()`), plus one tick a second while a call is in flight - so an open branch, a scroll offset and a text selection in either one survive everything else the stream is doing. `mount()` restores the scroll offset of an element carrying an `id` and of nothing else, which is why no block of text in a panel is a scroller of its own: `.code` grows and the panel around it scrolls, and the single exception is the viewer's `.jv-raw`, which carries an `id` for exactly that reason.
- Call record (`store.getCall(key)`): `rationale` / `rationale_truncated` are the `tool.call.started` copy (up to 8,192 chars), which the Call inspector, the now strip, the episode header (its first call's copy, `chain.js`) and the INSIDE audit hand-off card print, and which `rationaleNote` measures; REQUEST and the row's `+rationale` chip read the value off `call.arguments` instead; `rationale_declared` / `rationale_declared_truncated` are the gate's `intent.declared` record (1,024 chars); `http` is `{event_id, status, duration_ms}` of the `http.request` sharing the JSON-RPC id, never in `child_event_ids`, and `null` when no `http.request` carries that JSON-RPC id. Catalog record (`store.getCatalog(xs)`): `tools` come from the event's own array, else `getCatalogByHash(content_hash)`, else the retained event `snapshot_ref` names, else the session's previous catalog, else `[]`; `resolved_from` (`event` / `hash` / `snapshot_ref` / `previous` / `none`) says which and `event_id` is the event the array came from; entries are the recorded objects, untouched. `store.getCatalogByHash(hash)` returns `{tools, event_id, xs}` of the latest full listing with that hash, or `null`. `store.getCounters().id_collisions` counts a `tool.call.started` that reused a JSON-RPC id inside one `xs` (0 over the fixture); the newer call keeps the key.
- The three hops of one call (`hops.js`). `requestHop(call, session, {links, viewer, children | events, catalog})` -> `{title, rows, rationale, denied, observer, footer, rawEventId}`; `insideHop(call, children, {status})` -> `{summary, cards}`; `responseHop(call, {status, frozen, consumers})` -> `{title, rows, size, structured, error, http, took, ratios, port, footer, rawEventId, waiting}`. A row is `{path, key, value, actor, implied, redacted, port, emphasis}` with `value` as recorded, untouched, and `path` the place it sat in the envelope; a card is `{n, actor, title, in, out, source, event_id, note}`.
  - **Arrival order.** The request rows are read straight off `call.arguments`, in the order the keys arrived. `describeIn` (`chain.js`) is deliberately not used: it hoists a `from_date` / `to_date` pair into a synthetic `dates` part, which moves every later key - the `rationale` included - out of the position it actually arrived in, and this pane's whole claim is "as it arrived".
  - **Implied is never a recorded value.** `id`, `params.name`, the arguments and `params._meta` are stored fields; `method` is inferred from the event type, so its row is actor `page` with `implied: true`. `id` and `_meta` are the client app's (`agent`), `params.name` and every argument including `rationale` are the model's.
  - **Evidence rules.** The denied row (`the bearer gate answered before the body was read: no arguments were recorded`) is drawn only when the call is denied with no arguments and no rationale, and it sits on the server rail - no model wrote that sentence. The observer line is drawn off `redacted_fields` containing `arguments`, never off the viewer kind, which only chooses the wording (Decision D-5). The audit hand-off card needs a write tool, a rationale that was present *and* a `bank.op` carrying an `audit_id`; the classifier card needs an `intent.inferred` nested under the call. The gate card's "handed to" line is on the **page** rail because the arguments the handler received are recorded nowhere - the lenient schema fills its defaults in first, then `rationale` is stripped (`src/tools/registry.ts`).
  - **The size rule** (`sizeFacts(text_preview, content_chars, content_types)`): strip a trailing `…[truncated]` before counting, then clamp what was kept to what was sent, because a multi-block result joins its blocks with a newline and the preview can be longer than the summed `content_chars`. The row reads `486 sent · 69 kept`, `36 chars` when they are equal, `waiting` while the call is running and `no text` when the result carried no text block; the sentence never says the recording is complete while `kept < sent`, and adds `(the per-result cap)` when the producer did the cutting. The content row is labelled from `content_types` as `content · text (N block(s), joined)` and never `content[0]`: the stored preview is every text block joined.
  - **The rationale note rule.** The locator is a page-rail claim about the schema (`required by every published schema (ADR-8)` plus the listing id the schema was *not* recorded on at v0.4, or `required by the schema sent at tools/list #<id>` once the row carries a `descriptor`). The note is a server-rail record built from the `intent.declared` child, and its cap clause is derived from the two stored copies, never from `rationale_truncated` - that flag is set for anything over **1,024** characters (`isRationaleTruncated`, `src/contracts/tools.ts`), so it cannot tell "the handler got a shorter copy" (`the handler and the audit row kept the first 1,024 characters`) apart from "this server cut the record" (`cut by this server at 8,192 characters`). A call with no usable rationale gets the A-06 chip from `missingRationale` instead.
  - `ratios` are printed only at 5% or more, or when a cap bit is set, which only `content_chars` reaching `content_cap` (150,000) does, never the recording's 2,048-character preview cut; `took` carries no percentage. The JSON-RPC id is the only number for a call, and the classifier prints `scored 0.85`, never a percentage. `http` is two rows - the client app opened it, the server answered it - and only when the store attached one by JSON-RPC id; it is never matched by time. `port` names the call a value came from (`from #4 · table`) or went to (`to #5 · table`), built from the chain links.
- **The Possibility space** (`panel-possibility.js`, `ui.toolDescriptorCard`). An opened tool row shows the description verbatim and whole (`as sent with tools/list #<id>`), the parameter table, `annotations` and `_meta` as `key value` pairs in recorded order (an annotation `title` equal to the row's is not repeated), the digest, and a `raw descriptor` disclosure holding a `jsonView` with id `catViewerId(xs, name)`. The `rationale` row is `.who-model`; under it, on the dashed page rail, `answered by params.arguments.rationale on every tools/call · called N times in this session`, where N counts every call the store holds for that tool in that session, denied ones included, followed by `, M of them without a rationale (intent.missing, A-06)` when M of those calls carry an `intent.missing` (`load_accounts` in `xs_3f1c9a`: 1 of 1). Verdicts, from `view.schemaChecks['<listing id>/<tool>']`: `verified` "recorded schema is intact: re-hashes to the digest recorded beside it, computed in this browser"; `mismatch` "recorded schema does not re-hash to the digest recorded beside it (computed in this browser)"; `unavailable` "not checked: this browser has no crypto.subtle (it needs https or localhost)"; `failed` "not checked: this browser could not compute the digest"; no entry yet "checking the recorded schema". A closed row carries the short label with the sentence in its `title`, the open card the sentence itself. Only `mismatch` and the ADR-13 states (listed but not usable, hidden) are loud. No sentence speaks of what the client received or of the description, which no digest covers, and none says the model read anything (A-05). A row without a `descriptor` prints "This listing recorded name, title, flags, scopes and a schema digest only (contracts v0.4). The description and schema the client received were not captured." and the call count, and draws no verdict.
- JSON consumed (shapes in `src/contracts/xray-api.ts`): `ViewerMeResponse`, `XraySessionsResponse`, `XraySessionEventsResponse`, `XraySessionBankResponse` (into `view.bank = {xs, payload, error, fetchedAt, busy}`), `PairResponse`, SSE frames named `xray` carrying an `XrayEvent`, and the fixture JSONL.

## Known gaps
- `view.drawer` and `view.aside` are held, are in the repaint signatures and answer `close-drawer` / `toggle-aside`, but nothing draws either state yet (the Bytes drawer, D-19): the detail panel is still the six tabs and the aside is always full width. `toolKey` and the `tool`, `toolRaw` and `connInit` kinds are still unused; the connection block draws its steps as context rows, not the listing's tools.
- The Account's months control reads the server again; in sample mode only the recorded 3-month window exists, so the other buttons are disabled. The month chart's tick labels shrink below legible at phone width.
- The Overview's argument list and problem list come from the server's scan; over the recording `statsFromStore` counts only the 6,000 events the page holds, and its buckets follow the recording's own clock.
- A `mismatch`, `unavailable` or `failed` verdict is exercised only in `__tests__/panels.test.mjs`, never in the browser, and the verdict retention, the re-check of a re-list without rows and the generation guard in `app.js` are exercised by neither.
- The JSON-viewer probe in `_dev/check-console.mjs` ("an opened branch surviving a repaint") calls `window.__xray.render()`, which remounts nothing while the detail signature is unchanged, so it does not prove what it names; the Possibility probe forces a remount with a tab round trip instead.
- Only the view is deep-linked (`?view=`): `view.open` starts empty on every page view and is not written to the URL, so a reader cannot hand someone the call they are looking at.
- A manual reconnect cannot carry a cursor: `EventSource` sends `Last-Event-ID` only on its own retries. Correct but wasteful.
- The backfill stops at 2,000 events per session; older history is not shown.
